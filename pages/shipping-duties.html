What Does "Customs & Duties Pre-Paid (DDP)" Mean?

Unlike ordinary dropshipping stores where international customers get hit with unexpected 20–30% VAT bills and courier clearance admin fees upon delivery, FROSTBYTE operates 100% on a Delivered Duty Paid (DDP) basis.

Our Guarantee: All local import duties, VAT/GST, and customs brokerage fees are calculated and completely covered at checkout. You and the gift recipient will never be asked to pay another cent when the carrier rings the doorbell.
